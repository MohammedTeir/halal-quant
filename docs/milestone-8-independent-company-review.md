## One methodology and its application

Use the official **FTSE Yasaar Global Equity Shariah Index Series ground rules v4.6, February 2026**, sections 4.3.1, 4.3.2 and 4.4. The [official PDF](https://www.lseg.com/content/dam/ftse-russell/en_us/documents/ground-rules/ftse-yasaar-global-equity-shariah-index-series-ground-rules.pdf) was retrieved again; its SHA-256 remains `cb8273205a1276489fa2f984424c33d50cba3f5b56f5886c5b57a19d641cc021`.

The static conditions are debt/assets <33.333%, cash plus interest-bearing items/assets <33.333%, accounts receivable plus cash/assets <50%, and total interest plus non-compliant activity income/revenue ≤5%. Section 4.3.1 identifies conventional finance/insurance, intoxicants, pork/non-halal food, specified entertainment, tobacco and weapons/arms/defense manufacturing; its list is not exhaustive. General-purpose hardware, games, cloud services and advertising are not automatically classified as those prohibited activities. Direct prohibited offerings and related revenue need attribution; unresolved classifications do not become zero.

The following are **explicit research accounting conventions**, because the short public rules do not settle every accounting question:

- Use each latest eligible financial filing available in the retrieved SEC submissions as of November 2. Use its balance-sheet date and its current year-to-date income interval, or the annual interval for MU. Do not mix annual interest with quarterly revenue. These intervals are our transparent static research choice, not an asserted FTSE requirement.
- Use reported carrying debt, including drawn notes/loans, commercial paper and finance leases. Exclude operating leases, undrawn facilities, future contractual interest and alternative fair-value debt quotations. Reconcile principal/discount/hedge adjustments rather than add principal to carrying balances.
- Include cash, restricted cash, conventional debt investments and identified interest-bearing financing receivables. Remove overlaps with cash equivalents and separately identified equity. A mixed investment balance is not automatically all debt or all permissible equity.
- Count gross interest and identified positive conventional-debt returns without offsetting borrowing costs or unrelated losses. Positive debt-security gains are included in this conservative research application. Do not automatically classify every equity revaluation as prohibited income. Business-revenue and investee-income attribution still require source-backed classification.
- Receivables/cash calculations use reported net trade receivables, including the separately disclosed noncurrent MSFT balance. They do not replace trade receivables with every tax/vendor/other receivable. The table shows mapped disclosures, not a completeness attestation.

This is a static independent application. The rules' official constituent universe, Yasaar Board approval and two-successive-quarter monitoring/buffer process are not replicated. A static breach is not a claim that FTSE has deleted a constituent. The existing immutable campaign and its pending methodology have not been rewritten or approved.

## Purification and instrument evidence

Section 4.4 resolves the selected method's published dividend recommendation: **purification amount = 5% × eligible dividend cash actually received**. No company-specific impure-revenue percentage is substituted for this fixed rule. It does not make prohibited-sector or over-limit stocks eligible. The public paragraph is not a complete policy for disposing of failed holdings or capital gains; those decisions require actual qualified adoption and review.

For the **internal simulator**, ownership means virtual cash, virtual holdings and simulated settlement checked through the ledger and its replay/reconciliation invariants. Real custody statements and actual dividend receipts are **live-readiness evidence**, not prerequisites for virtual entries. The simulator must still bind the candidate symbol/share class to its protocol and enforce owned-share sales, cash availability, settlement and its existing policy/review gates. Software tests do not establish an actual elapsed campaign.

The current SEC cover pages support common-stock identity, including **GOOGL Class A versus GOOG Class C**. The [SEC T+1 adoption release](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-29) confirms the May 28, 2024 compliance date. These public facts inform instrument identity and simulated settlement; they do not establish real custody.

For **live readiness only**, exact missing account facts include the account-bound security identifier/contract, custody/beneficial ownership, settled-share/cash evidence, share-class entitlement, holdings on relevant dates and dividend cash actually received. Public issuer declarations can establish per-share distributions, but cannot establish private holdings or receipts. Operator-provided documents or an authorized read-only account record would be needed for those live facts. No receipt amount or missing zero is fabricated. The selected methodology's purification/disposition policy still needs qualified application; actual live dividend cash is not a paper-entry requirement.

**Full independent financial and business screening before virtual entries remains required.** Correcting the ownership scope does not approve any candidate, waive a financial/business unknown or start the campaign.
