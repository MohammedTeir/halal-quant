# Compliance policy specification

Status: **hypothetical control checks implemented; operational compliance authority is not yet implemented**. See [Milestone 5 increment](milestone-5-controls.md). No
instrument is approved by this repository. The screening methodology, qualified
review process, provider and gold instrument remain undecided.

Asset assessments must record methodology, provider, evidence, effective/expiry
dates and purification guidance. Stocks require business activity and financial
ratio checks. ETFs require mandate, holdings process, structure and supervision
checks. Gold requires documented ownership, possession, custody, settlement and
redemption arrangements; an XAUUSD chart is insufficient evidence.

Only a current APPROVED assessment may authorize a new purchase. PENDING,
REJECTED, missing, expired or unverified assessments block purchases. A status
change on an owned asset triggers review under the selected scholarly divestment
policy; automatic indefinite holding or immediate liquidation is not assumed.

Transaction checks must reject interest-bearing margin, shorts, borrowed shares,
conventional CFDs/futures/options, unowned or unsellable quantities, unsupported
fractional custody, invalid possession/settlement, and prohibited securities
lending. Purchases must be cash-funded. Broker evidence must be authoritative;
AI output and user-supplied signal payloads cannot establish ownership or approval.

Milestone 5 acceptance must cover each forbidden case, stale assessments,
insufficient ownership and instrument-specific restrictions. Historical research
must use assessments available at the simulated time. Independent compliance
review remains a prerequisite for any live-readiness approval.
